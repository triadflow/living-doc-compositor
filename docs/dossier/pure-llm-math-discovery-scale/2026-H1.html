The beginning of the serious scale.

This page defines a high-end complexity scale for general LLM reasoning without external tools: no theorem prover, no code execution, no search engine, no Lean, no brute-force construction search.

Rene Luijk · Jun 6, 2026 · 7 min read · framework mathematics reasoning AI science
TL;DR

The unit-distance result is not the end of the scale. It is the beginning of the serious scale.

If Level 1 is real for pure/general LLM reasoning, then the core question becomes whether Levels 2–8 scale smoothly, or whether there is a hard wall around Levels 8–9 where “taste”, framework invention, and theory construction become qualitatively different.

The baseline is the reported OpenAI result on the Erdős unit-distance conjecture: an internal general-purpose reasoning model generated a counterexample to a long-standing conjecture in discrete geometry.1Note 1OpenAI's announcement is dated May 20, 2026 and describes an internal model disproving a longstanding conjecture in the planar unit-distance problem. OpenAI says the result came from a general-purpose reasoning model rather than a system trained specifically for mathematics or scaffolded to search proof strategies.2Note 2The boundary matters: this scale is about pure/general LLM reasoning without external theorem provers, code execution, search, Lean, or brute-force construction search. The companion remarks paper says the proof was “first mathematically generated in one shot” by an internal OpenAI model, then refined expositionally with human/Codex interaction.3Note 3The companion source is Alon–Bloom–Gowers–Litt–Sawin–Shankar–Tsimerman–Wang–Wood, Remarks on the disproof of the unit distance conjecture.

The 12-level scale

This first table starts where ordinary AI-math discussions usually end. Level 1 is already real research discovery.

LevelComplexity levelWhat the model achievesWhy this is higher
1Famous-conjecture counterexampleFinds a valid counterexample to a long-standing conjecture.Baseline: original research discovery, not benchmark math.
2Deep-synthesis counterexampleFinds a counterexample using several distant mathematical ideas.Requires fusing remote concepts, not one local trick.
3New theorem proofProves a previously open theorem in a narrow research area.A proof must close every logical gap.
4Hidden-lemma chainInvents several intermediate lemmas not obvious from the statement.Requires proof architecture, not just final-step insight.
5Long publishable proofProduces a paper-level proof that survives expert review.Requires rigor across many dependencies.
6Cross-field proofSolves a problem by importing machinery from another field.Requires recognizing bridges between mathematical languages.
7Multiple related open problemsSolves a cluster of connected problems.Shows a general mechanism, not a one-off solution.
8New methodCreates a reusable proof technique or construction method.The output becomes a tool for future mathematics.
9New frameworkInvents definitions or abstractions that reorganize a subfield.Changes the representation of the problem space.
10Theory-building sequenceProduces definitions, lemmas, theorems, examples, applications.Requires sustained mathematical taste and direction.
11Major central conjectureSolves a field-shaping problem.Must overcome many deep failed approaches.
12Field creationCreates a new mathematical language or domain.Expands what mathematics can naturally express.

Compression: counterexample → deep counterexample → theorem → lemma-chain proof → long paper → cross-field proof → problem cluster → method → framework → research program → central conjecture → new field.

Current evidence

This table asks: what properties do current models seem to show already, and what is probably missing for the next level?

LevelComplexity levelCurrent evidence in pure/general LLMsLikely missing capability for next level
1Famous-conjecture counterexampleDemonstrated / strongly indicated by the OpenAI unit-distance result.Reliable transfer across multiple math domains.
2Deep-synthesis counterexampleEarly signs. Models can combine distant concepts, but reliability is unclear.Robust abstraction matching.
3New theorem proofPartial signs. Strong proof sketches exist, but hidden gaps remain common.Global proof consistency.
4Hidden-lemma chainWeak-to-emerging signs. Lemma invention appears, but often unstable.Hierarchical proof planning.
5Long publishable proofLimited evidence. Research notes that LLMs still struggle with long proofs and complex formalizations.Long-range dependency management.
6Cross-field proofRare signs. Some surprising analogies appear, but not dependable.Field-independent structural representations.
7Multiple related open problemsNot clearly demonstrated. Successes remain problem-centric.Discovery of general mechanisms.
8New methodVery early signs. Some constructions look reusable.Optimization for generality and reusability.
9New frameworkAlmost no evidence. Models invent definitions, but rarely enduring ones.Mathematical taste.
10Theory-building sequenceNo convincing evidence.Sustained autonomous intellectual direction.
11Major central conjectureNo evidence yet.All prior capabilities plus extreme reliability.
12Field creationNo evidence yet.Creation of new conceptual languages humans adopt.

If generalized to science

This final table assumes the mathematical capability at each level generalizes to physics, biology, chemistry, economics, computer science, medicine, and other scientific domains.

LevelComplexity levelIf generalized beyond mathematics, this opens up...
1Famous-conjecture counterexampleFinding flaws in accepted models; discovering overlooked mechanisms in biology, economics, materials, and climate science.
2Deep-synthesis counterexampleUnexpected bridges between fields: ecology into economics, condensed matter into neuroscience, information theory into medicine.
3New theorem proofSolving well-defined scientific questions where the ingredients already exist but were not connected correctly.
4Hidden-lemma chainDiscovery of missing intermediate mechanisms in gene networks, disease pathways, cognition, climate, or complex systems.
5Long publishable proofEnd-to-end scientific explanations spanning hundreds of interacting factors: whole-cell models, aging mechanisms, causal climate models.
6Cross-field proofTrue interdisciplinary breakthroughs where one field unlocks another.
7Multiple related open problemsSolving whole classes of scientific puzzles at once rather than isolated cases.
8New methodNew experimental, computational, or theoretical methods used by thousands of researchers.
9New frameworkNew paradigms comparable in role to evolutionary theory, information theory, plate tectonics, or game theory.
10Theory-building sequenceAutonomous research programs producing years of coherent scientific progress.
11Major central conjectureProgress on civilization-scale bottlenecks: fusion theory, room-temperature superconductivity theory, aging, consciousness, climate control.
12Field creationEntirely new sciences or conceptual languages humans had not yet formulated.
